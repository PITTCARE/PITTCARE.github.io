External advisory board

Guidance through relationship, not bureaucracy

Independent experts, external to the University, who meet individually with the center director once a year and convene together each summer. No committees — just sustained, expert perspective.

Annual 1:1Each member meets the center director once a year — open conversation, no fixed agenda.
Summer meetingThe full board convenes alongside research faculty and collaborators; hears Voice of the People findings; sets direction for the year ahead.
IndependenceMembers are external to Pitt by design — their distance is what lets them say what internal colleagues can't.
