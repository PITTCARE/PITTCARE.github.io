Ethics & responsible AI governance

Ethical review as a standing practice

Grounded in the WHO's global guidance on AI in health — six principles carried into every project, not checked once at launch.

AutonomyPatients and clinicians keep control over medical decisions.
Well-being & SafetyTools meet regulatory standards; avoidable harm is prevented.
TransparencyHow a tool reasons is explainable to the people who rely on it.
AccountabilityResponsibility for outcomes rests with named people.
EquityPerformance is monitored for bias across populations.
SustainabilitySystems stay responsive as evidence and technology evolve.

This pillar is grounded in a formal partnership with Pitt's Institute for Bioethics, led by inaugural director Douglas B. White, MD, MAS, and is made visible each year through the Voice of the People Survey above.
